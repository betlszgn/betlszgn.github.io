About Me

The most important thing in a person's life is finding their passion and living according to it. I am aware that this is not easy for most of us. I, for instance, struggled with finding my passion for a long time, and there was no one big, clear answer.

I aimed to have a bigger purpose, and then I decided that studying physics would give me that big purpose. But later on, I realized I was not meant to study a theoretical subject since it was not applicable to my character. Later on, after trying different possessions, I realized that I was never meant to study a theoretical subject but to do something in a more practical field. For instance, creating any kind of art made me exited then I could ever imagine.

"The unexamined life is not worth living." Socrates
