About Me

The most important thing in a person's life is finding their passion and living according to it. I am aware that this is not easy for most of us. I, for instance, struggled with finding my passion for a long time, and there was no one big, clear answer.

I aimed to have a bigger purpose, and then I decided that pursueing a PhD in physics would give me that big purpose. But later on, I realized I was not meant to study a theoretical subject since it was not applicable to my character. I tried different possessions,and I realized that I was never meant to study a theoretical subject but to do something in a more practical field. For instance, creating any kind of art is making me exited then I could ever imagine.

This idea connects with Aristotle’s view of aretē, or excellence, which suggests that a fulfilling life comes from developing ourselves according to our own nature and abilities.

“The good of man comes to be ‘a working of the soul in the way of excellence.’” Aristotle
